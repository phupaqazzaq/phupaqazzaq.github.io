QS 2027 #345 · AUN-QA

A degree respected beyond Thailand

Mahidol University is ranked #345* in the world and #55 in Asia (QS Asia 2026). The ICT programme is assessed under AUN-QA, the ASEAN University Network's quality standard.

FOR PARENTSFour years invested in a qualification that employers and universities across ASEAN recognise.

IELTS 5.0 · DUOLINGO 80

Every class taught in English

The B.Sc. in ICT is an international programme taught in English, open to Thai and international students. Start from IELTS 5.0 or Duolingo 80 and graduate with professional English for a global career.

FOR PARENTSEnglish-medium study, with a structured path to improve along the way.

90,000 × 8 = 720,000 THB

One flat fee, no surprises

As ASEAN neighbours, we believe a great education should be within reach. Tuition is 90,000 THB per semester and stays the same for all four years, with no per-credit charges.

FOR PARENTSYou know the full tuition cost from day one, even when sending money from home is difficult.

75 STUDENTS · 7 COUNTRIES

Your launchpad to internships abroad

Last year, 75 ICT Mahidol students interned at 17 partner universities and institutions in 7 countries, supported by 5.66 million THB in scholarships and funding (AY2025). Scholarships are competitive, so apply early.
