Check these four entry rules

These are the main rules for 2027. Test scores must be from the two years before you apply.

  • School

    Grade 12 (US curriculum), Cambridge AS/A-Level, the IB or GED, finished or in progress

  • Grades

    High school CGPA of at least 2.50, or the equivalent

  • English

    One test, such as IELTS 5.0, Duolingo 80 or TOEFL iBT 60

  • Maths

    One test, such as SAT Math 600, ACT Math 25 or IGCSE Extended grade B

Taking the Myanmar school exam? It is not on the published list, so please ask Admissions which scores you need before you apply.
